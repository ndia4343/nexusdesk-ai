# HR Policy: Employee Handbook & Operations

## Annual Leave, Paid Time Off (PTO) & Statutory Holidays
Our company believes in work-life integration and mandatory rest periods:
- Annual PTO Accrual: Full-time employees accrue 18 days of paid vacation leave per calendar year, accrued semi-monthly on the 1st and 15th (0.75 days per pay period).
- Company Holidays: 10 statutory paid holidays per year plus 2 floating personal cultural days.
- Rollover Policy: Employees may carry over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the subsequent calendar year; rollover days must be utilized before March 31st or are forfeited.
- Sick Leave: 10 dedicated sick leave days are front-loaded annually on January 1st (not deducted from vacation PTO). Consecutive absences exceeding 3 business days require a medical certificate.
- Request Procedures: Vacation requests exceeding 3 consecutive days must be submitted via BambooHR/Workday at least two weeks in advance for departmental manager approval.

## Hybrid & Remote Work Operations Guidelines
We operate on a trust-centric flexible hybrid schedule:
- Core Days: Employees in hybrid roles attend the office 3 core days per week (Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday) with flexible remote work on Mondays and Fridays.
- Fully Remote Tier: Employees hired into approved remote designations are exempt from office presence but are expected to attend quarterly team offsites with company-covered travel.
- Working Hours: Core collaboration hours across all time zones are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.
- Home Office Setup Stipend: New full-time hires receive a one-time, non-taxable $500 home workspace allowance for ergonomic chairs, external monitors, or standing desks within their first 60 days.

## Business Expenses, Travel & Reimbursement Policy
Guidelines for submitting business expenses through the company finance portal (Concur/Expensify):
- Meal Per Diem Limits: Domestic travel meal allowance is capped at $75 per day ($15 breakfast, $25 lunch, $35 dinner). Alcohol is non-reimbursable unless part of an authorized client entertainment dinner.
- Receipt Threshold: Itemized receipts are mandatory for all business expenses exceeding $25. Credit card summary slips alone are not accepted by internal auditing.
- Flight & Lodging Bookings: Commercial flights must be booked in Economy/Coach via the corporate travel desk. Hotel stays are reimbursed up to $220/night in standard metropolitan areas and $295/night in tier-1 financial hubs (SF, NYC, London).
- Submission Deadline: Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense; reports submitted after 60 days require VP Finance written authorization.

## IT Hardware Provisioning, Cybersecurity & Device Replacement
Protecting corporate assets and engineering infrastructure:
- Hardware Allocation: Engineering and product staff receive an Apple MacBook Pro 16" (M3/M4 Pro, 36GB RAM) or Lenovo ThinkPad P1 workstation; corporate and operations staff receive MacBook Air 15" or Dell XPS 15.
- Multi-Factor Authentication: Okta Verify with FIDO2 WebAuthn (Touch ID or YubiKey) is enforced across all single sign-on enterprise applications. SMS-based 2FA is prohibited.
- Password Management: All employees are provided an enterprise 1Password vault; master passwords must meet 16-character minimum entropy standards.
- Hardware Refresh Cycle: Company-issued primary laptops are refreshed every 36 months. Upon refresh, employees may purchase their deprecated machine at fair market salvage value ($150) after certified secure IT wipe.

## Healthcare, Retirement & Employee Wellness Benefits
Comprehensive total rewards program:
- Health, Vision & Dental Coverage: Premium PPO and High-Deductible Health Plans (HDHP) with 85% employer-subsidized employee premiums and 70% dependent premiums, active on the 1st of the month following start date.
- 401(k) Retirement Match: 100% employer match up to the first 4% of eligible salary, immediately vested upon contribution completion after 90 days of employment.
- Monthly Wellness Allowance: $50 monthly lifestyle stipend reimbursable for gym memberships, fitness trackers, meditation apps (Headspace/Calm), or mental health therapy copays.
- Employee Assistance Program (EAP): Confidential 24/7 counseling support offering 6 free therapy sessions per year per life event for employees and immediate household family members.

## Parental Leave, Caregiver Support & Bereavement
Family-first leave protections for major life milestones:
- Primary Caregiver Leave: 16 weeks of 100% fully paid parental leave for birthing, non-birthing, adoptive, or foster parents, usable anytime within the child's first 12 months.
- Secondary Caregiver Leave: 6 weeks of fully paid leave.
- Phased Return-to-Work: Parents returning from parental leave can work 80% hours at 100% pay for their first 4 weeks back.
- Bereavement Leave: Up to 5 consecutive paid days for immediate family members (spouse, child, parent, sibling); 3 days for extended family.
