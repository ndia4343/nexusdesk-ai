# ApexCare Health & Specialist Clinic — Patient Operations & Clinical Policies

## General Consultations & Specialist Pricing
ApexCare Clinic provides comprehensive outpatient family medicine and specialist consultations:
- Standard Primary Care Consultation: $85 flat fee (covered under eligible insurance copays).
- Specialized Consultations (Cardiology, Dermatology, Orthopedics): $175 initial evaluation; $110 follow-up visit.
- Urgent Care & Same-Day Walk-In Triage: $125 facility triage fee.
- Preventative Annual Physical & Wellness Exams: 100% covered under ACA-compliant commercial insurance plans without copay.
Self-pay patients receive a 20% prompt-pay discount when settling invoices on the date of clinical service.

## Insurance Coverage, Copays & Network Participation
ApexCare participates in-network with major national and regional healthcare payers:
- In-Network Carriers: Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, Cigna, UnitedHealthcare, Medicare Part B, and Humana Choice.
- Out-of-Network Claims: We provide itemized CMS-1500 Superbills for patient self-submission and out-of-network reimbursement.
- Copayments & Deductibles: Specialist copays (typically $25-$50 depending on your plan tier) are collected at check-in prior to seeing the physician.
- Prior Authorization: Diagnostic imaging (MRI, CT scans) and specialty medications require 3 to 5 business days for insurer pre-certification.
- HSA/FSA Eligibility: All consultation fees, diagnostic procedures, lab draws, and durable medical equipment are fully HSA/FSA eligible.

## Appointment Scheduling, Cancellations & No-Show Policy
We respect our clinicians' and patients' time through a standardized booking policy:
- Cancellation Notice: We require at least 24 hours advance notice to reschedule or cancel an appointment without penalty.
- Late Cancellation / No-Show Fee: A $35 charge is billed directly to the patient for cancellations made under 24 hours or missed appointments. This fee cannot be billed to insurance.
- Arrival Window: New patients must arrive 15 minutes before their scheduled appointment time to complete biometric check-in and medical history verification.
- Late Arrivals: Patients arriving more than 15 minutes past their appointment time may be offered a shortened visit or rescheduled to protect subsequent appointment slots.

## Post-Procedure & Treatment Aftercare Guidelines
Following in-clinic minor surgeries, biopsies, cryotherapy, or joint injections:
- Dressing & Wound Care: Keep wound dressings clean and dry for 24 to 48 hours. Clean with sterile saline or mild unscented soap; apply prescribed topical antibiotic ointment.
- Activity Restrictions: Avoid heavy lifting (> 15 lbs), strenuous cardiovascular exercise, swimming pools, and hot tubs for 72 hours post-procedure.
- Pain Management: Mild discomfort can be managed with acetaminophen (500mg every 6 hours) or ibuprofen (400mg every 8 hours with food), unless contraindicated by your physician.
- Emergency Warning Signs: Contact our on-call triage line immediately or proceed to the nearest emergency department if you experience fever > 101°F (38.3°C), spreading erythema, persistent purulent drainage, or uncontrolled bleeding.

## Prescription Refills & Diagnostic Lab Results
Managing ongoing patient medications and lab turnaround:
- Routine Prescription Refills: Request refills directly through the ApexCare Patient Portal or your dispensing pharmacy at least 48 hours prior to running out.
- Controlled Substances: Schedule II-IV medications require an in-person or telehealth clinical evaluation every 90 days and cannot be refilled early.
- Diagnostic Lab Turnaround: Standard blood panels (CBC, Lipid, Metabolic) are processed within 24 to 48 hours and automatically published to your portal with clinician commentary.
- Specialized Pathology & Biopsy: Results typically return in 5 to 7 business days; a clinical nurse will contact you directly to schedule a review consultation.

## Telehealth & Virtual Consultations
ApexCare Virtual Desk offers remote clinical care for eligible acute and chronic conditions:
- Eligible Conditions: Routine follow-ups, medication reviews, lab result discussions, seasonal allergies, mild upper respiratory infections, and dermatological rashes.
- Technical Requirements: Secure video visits are conducted through the HIPAA-compliant ApexCare Portal on iOS, Android, or desktop web browsers with a high-definition webcam and microphone.
- Licensing Jurisdiction: Patients must be physically located in the state of provider licensure at the time of the virtual consultation.
